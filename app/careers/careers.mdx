## How we work

| Principle | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Small team | Few people, each owning real parts of the product |
| Ship, then improve | We release early and fix in public |
| Focused work | Make deliberate choices about scope and priorities |
| Reliability first | Core systems are built in Rust and meant to run for years |
| Premium and secure | Quality and security are not a later step |

## Voice talent

Miralas depends on real voices. If you are an Uzbek speaker with a clear, pleasant voice and you are interested in working with us, we would like to hear from you.

When you write, please include:

1. A short voice sample, recorded in a quiet room.
2. The languages you speak.
3. A little about yourself and your experience, if any.

We only work with voices we have the right to use, under clear written agreements.
