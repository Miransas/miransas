# Contact

We are a small team, and we read what you send us. Pick the option that fits best so your message reaches the right place faster.

---

## Who to write to

| Topic | Where |
| --- | --- |
| General questions, partnerships | [contact@miransas.com](mailto:contact@miransas.com) |
| Help with a product | [Support](/support) |
| Security reports | [contact@miransas.com](mailto:contact@miransas.com) with the subject "Security" |
| Voice talent for Miralas | [contact@miransas.com](mailto:contact@miransas.com) with the subject "Voice" |
| Code and issues | [github.com/Miransas](https://github.com/Miransas) |
| Product updates | [Miransas News](/news) and the [RSS feed](/news/rss.xml) |

---

## Before you write

1. **Product help:** Check [Support](/support) first. Many answers are already there.
2. **Be specific.** Tell us which product, what you expected and what happened. Screenshots help.
3. **Never send secrets.** Do not include passwords, API keys or tokens in a message. If you pasted one by mistake, tell us and rotate it.

---

## Security reports

If you found a vulnerability, write to us with the subject "Security" and give us a chance to fix it before you share details publicly. Please include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what you think the impact is.

---

## Voice talent

If you are an Uzbek speaker interested in working on Miralas, write with the subject "Voice" and attach a short sample recorded in a quiet room. More details are on the [Careers](/careers) page.
