## How we choose what to build

**One thing at a time.** The current order of work puts the Miralas core before the Voice Agent. Pausing a project can be a deliberate engineering decision.

**Own the important layers.** Developer tools and infrastructure are a recurring part of the project map, alongside voice and native software.

**Keep a public record.** [The project history](/news/built-so-far) and [Miralas development notes](/news/miralas-status) explain the work in its original context.

**Work within clear boundaries.** Read [What we will not build](/news/principles) for the principles behind those choices.
