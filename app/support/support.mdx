## Frequently asked questions

<details>
<summary>Can I use Miralas today?</summary>

Not yet. Miralas is in development. We plan to open a free beta first so early users can try it and give feedback, and paid plans come after that. Announcements will appear on [Miransas News](/news).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Does Miralas support Uzbek?</summary>

Yes, Uzbek is the main goal of the product. We are training our own Uzbek model, because most existing tools do not support the language well. Other language capabilities depend on the selected open-source model and its release. You can read the details on the [models page](/models).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where do I sign in to Miralas?</summary>

Sign-in will live on the Miralas console. The console is not public yet. Use the project overview and News for availability updates.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is my voice data safe?</summary>

Our product direction favors self-hosted voice processing. Read the current [privacy policy](https://privacy.miransas.com/legal/privacy) for published terms; a recorded homepage demonstration is not a data-processing service.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Can I clone any voice?</summary>

No. Only clone your own voice or a voice you have written permission to use. Using cloning for impersonation, fraud or misleading content is not allowed. Read more in [What we will not build](/news/principles).

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is the Voice Agent available?</summary>

Not yet. The Voice Agent comes after the Miralas core is finished. See the [agent page](/agent) for what it will do.

</details>

<details>
<summary>I found a security problem. What should I do?</summary>

Please write to us through the [contact page](/contact) with the subject "Security", and give us time to fix it before you share details publicly.

</details>

## Contacting support

When you write, include:

1. Which product you are using.
2. What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
3. Screenshots or error messages, if you have them.
4. Your browser or operating system, for website problems.

<Callout type="warning">
  Never send passwords, API keys or tokens in a message. If you shared one by mistake, tell us and
  rotate it right away.
</Callout>

---

## Response times

We are a small team. We read every message, and product and security issues come first, but we cannot promise a reply time for each one.
