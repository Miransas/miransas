Before Miralas, there was a long list of products built under the Miransas name. Here is an honest look at where each one stands today.

## The product map

| Project | What it is | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |

## A few lessons

1. **Native beats heavy.** Pulse is a small native app, not an Electron wrapper, and it feels like it.
2. **Own your infrastructure.** Binboi moved providers more than once. Because we kept the stack portable, each move was a migration, not a rewrite.
3. **Pause on purpose.** CourierX works. We paused it to focus, and it is easier to resume a paused project than to rescue a rushed one.
