Saying no is part of building a good company. These are the lines we have drawn for Miransas and Miralas.

## Our rules

| Rule | Why |
| --- | --- |
| No gambling, betting, casino or game-ad businesses as customers or sponsors | We will not put the Miransas or Miralas logo under the same roof as them. |
| No donation or payment-handling features | Handling card data invites fraud, and it carries banking and cybersecurity regulation we do not want to take on. |
| Voice cloning must be traceable | Cloning can be abused. Miralas is designed so that misuse can be traced back to an account. |
| Terms and policies written with legal counsel | A premium product needs premium paperwork. |

## What this means for Miralas

Early on, we considered a feature that reads donations aloud on streams. We decided against it permanently. Miralas stays focused on what we do best: voice cloning, text-to-speech and speech-to-speech.

## Voice cloning and responsibility

Cloning a voice is powerful, and it can be misused for fraud or impersonation. We take three steps:

1. We only work with voices we have the right to use, under clear agreements.
2. Our models and tools are used under terms that forbid impersonation and misleading content.
3. Our privacy policy, which we are preparing with legal counsel, will explain how account information is handled and when it can be disclosed.
