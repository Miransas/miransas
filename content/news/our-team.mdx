Miransas started as a one-person studio: a single developer building and selling SaaS products independently. The rules were simple. Ship fast, sell, reinvest, and never depend on outside investment.

That model got us far, but voice products need more than code. They need real people. So this year the team grew.

## Why voice actors?

Miralas, our voice AI platform, is only as good as the voices behind it. We do not scrape voices from the internet and we do not clone people without their consent. We work with real voice talent, recruited through our own network, under clear agreements.

<Callout type="info">
  A good Uzbek voice is the hardest part of the whole project. Every other language has open-source
  options. Uzbek needs people.
</Callout>

## What has not changed

1. We are still bootstrapped. No outside investment.
2. We still build our core systems in Rust wherever reliability matters.
3. Miransas, as the parent company, stays premium, high quality and secure.
